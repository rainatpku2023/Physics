### 从质点体系到场
+ **质点：**

 $ S=\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}L(q_{i},\dot{q}_{i},t)dt \quad (i=1,\cdot\cdot\cdot,f) $  
  $ \delta S=0\Rightarrow\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_{i}}-\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot{q}_{i}}=0 $

+ **从质点到场：**
    - $ i,t\rightarrow x_{\mu},t $
    - $ q_{i}(t)\rightarrow\varphi(x_{\mu}) $
    - $ \dot{q}_{i}(t)\rightarrow \partial_{\mu}\varphi(x_{\mu}) $
+ **定义拉氏密度** $ \mathcal{L}(\varphi(x), \partial_{\mu}\varphi(x)) $，**拉氏量：**

  $ L=\int d^{3}x\mathcal{L}(\varphi(x),\partial_{\mu}\varphi(x)) $  
  $ S=\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}dt~L=\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}dt~d^{3}x\mathcal{L}(\varphi(x),\partial_{\mu}\varphi(x)) $

> **注：** $ dt~d^{3}x=dt^{\prime}d^{3}x^{\prime} $ (洛伦兹标量)，L: 洛伦兹标量。
>

$ \delta S=\delta\int_{t_{1}}^{t_{2}}dt\int d^{3}x\mathcal{L}=\int d^{4}x~\delta\mathcal{L} $  
$ =\int d^{4}x[\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\varphi}\delta\psi+\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)}\delta(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)] \quad \text{其中 } \delta(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)=\partial_{\mu}\delta\varphi $  
$ =\int d^{4}x[\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\varphi}-\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{\mu}}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)}]\delta\varphi+\int d^{4}x\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{\mu}}[\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)}\delta\varphi] $  
$ =\int d^{4}x[\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\varphi}-\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{\mu}}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)}]\delta\varphi=0 $

+ **拉氏方程** $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\varphi}-\partial_{\mu}\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)}\right)=0 $
+ **定义：广义动量**

  $ \pi\equiv\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi)} $

+ 定义：哈密顿量密度

$ \mathcal{H}=\pi\dot{\varphi}-\mathcal{L} \quad (\dot{\varphi}\equiv\partial_{\mu}\varphi)\circ\varphi(x) $



+   $ \varphi(x) $ 可以是标量场，也可以是矢量场 $ \varphi_{r}(x), r=1,\cdot\cdot\cdot,n $

  $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\varphi_{r}}-\partial_{\mu}\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\varphi_{r})}\right)=0 \quad (r=1,\cdot\cdot\cdot,n) $

+ **例：矢量场** $ A_{\mu}(x): $

  $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial A_{\nu}}-\partial_{\mu}\left[\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}\right]=0 \quad (\nu=1,\cdot\cdot\cdot,4) $

### 电磁场的拉式量及运动方程
$ S=\int d^{4}x\mathcal{L} $

> L: 洛伦兹标量
>

+ 拉氏密度可分为自由空间电磁场（自由光子场）和相互作用项（带电粒子与电磁场相互作用）：

$ \mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{f}+\mathcal{L}_{int} $  
$ \mathcal{L}_{f}=-\frac{1}{4\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu} $

+ **拉格朗日方程：**

  
  $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial A_{\nu}}-\partial_{\mu}\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}\right)=0 $

    - 代入：  $ F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu} $

$ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}_{f}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}=-\frac{1}{4\mu_{0}}\frac{\partial(F_{\alpha\beta}F_{\alpha\beta})}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}=-\frac{1}{2\mu_{0}}F_{\alpha\beta}\frac{\partial F_{\alpha\beta}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})} $

+ **场和电荷的相互作用：**

  $ =-\frac{1}{2\mu_{0}}F_{\alpha\beta}(\delta_{\alpha\mu}\delta_{\beta\nu}-\delta_{\beta\mu}\delta_{\alpha\nu}) $  
  $ =-\frac{1}{2\mu_{0}}(F_{\mu\nu}-F_{\nu\mu})=-\frac{1}{\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu} $  
  $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}_{f}}{\partial A_{\nu}}=0; \quad \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}_{f}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}=-\frac{1}{\mu_{0}}F^{\mu\nu} $  
  $ \Rightarrow\partial_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}=0 $

+ **从单粒子的拉氏量到场的拉氏密度：**
    - **根据单粒子相互作用项推出电荷密度电流密度与场相互作用：**

  $ qA_{\mu}U_{\mu}/\gamma\rightarrow A_{\mu}\rho U_{\mu}/\gamma=A_{\mu}j_{\mu} $

    - ；拉格朗日量与拉格朗日方程：：

  
  $ \mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{4\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}+j_{\mu}A_{\mu} $  
  $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial A_{\nu}}-\partial_{\mu}\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}\right)=0 $

    - 求解拉格朗日方程：

  $ \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial A_{\nu}}=j_{\nu}, \quad \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu})}=-\frac{1}{\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu} $  
  $ \therefore \partial_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}=-\mu_{0}j_{\nu} \quad (\nu=1,2,3,4) $

    - 此外由 $ F_{\mu\nu} $ 定义有 $ \partial_{\mu}F_{\nu\lambda}+\partial_{\nu}F_{\lambda\mu}+\partial_{\lambda}F_{\mu\nu}=0 $，与上式共同构成协变形式的麦克斯韦方程组。
+ 
+ 4-势的波动方程（达朗贝尔方程）：

$ \partial_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}=-\mu_{0}j_{\nu} $  
$ \partial_{\mu}(\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu})=-\mu_{0}j_{\nu} $  
$ \Box A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}\partial_{\mu}A_{\mu}=-\mu_{0}j_{\nu} \quad (\Box=\partial_{\mu}\partial_{\mu}) $

  
取洛伦兹规范 $ \partial_{\mu}A_{\mu}=0 $  
$ \Rightarrow\Box A_{\nu}=-\mu_{0}j_{\nu} $

    - 可得协变形式的达朗贝尔方程。

### 规范不变性
+ **规范变换：**  $ A_{\mu}\rightarrow A_{\mu}^{\prime}=A_{\mu}+\partial_{\mu}\Lambda $
    - 规范变换下电磁场张量不变：

  $ F_{\mu\nu}\rightarrow F_{\mu\nu}^{\prime}=\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}^{\prime}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu}^{\prime} $  
  $ =\partial_{\mu}(A_{\nu}+\partial_{\nu}\Lambda)-\partial_{\nu}(A_{\mu}+\partial_{\mu}\Lambda) $  
  $ =\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu}=F_{\mu\nu} $

> $ F_{\mu\nu} $ 在规范变换下不变。
>

    - 规范变换下拉格朗日量密度变化：

$ \mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{4\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}+j_{\mu}A_{\mu} $  
$ \mathcal{L}\rightarrow\mathcal{L}^{\prime}=\mathcal{L}+j_{\mu}\partial_{\mu}\Lambda $

    - 规范变换下作用量只是增加一个表面项：

  
$ S=\int d^{4}x\mathcal{L}\rightarrow S^{\prime}=S+\int d^{4}x~j_{\mu}\partial_{\mu}\Lambda $  
$ =S+\int d^{4}x\partial_{\mu}(j_{\mu}\Lambda)-\int d^{4}x~\Lambda\partial_{\mu}j_{\mu} =S+\text{boundary term} $

        * 第二行用到了$ \partial_{\mu}j_{\mu}=0 $。
    - 作用量在规范变化下不变；$ \delta S=0 $ 不变。此外，场方程 $ \partial_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}^{\prime}=\partial_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}=-\mu_{0}j_{\nu} $ 在规范变换下不变，同时：

$ \partial_{\mu}F_{\nu\lambda}^{\prime}+\partial_{\nu}F_{\lambda\mu}^{\prime}+\partial_{\lambda}F_{\mu\nu}^{\prime}=\partial_{\mu}F_{\nu\lambda}+\partial_{\nu}F_{\lambda\mu}+\partial_{\lambda}F_{\mu\nu}=0 $

    - 规范变换不改变 Maxwell 方程。观测到的物理在规范变换下不变，规范不变性与电荷守恒兼容。

### 光子的质量
+ **电磁场的拉氏密度：**

  $ \mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{f}+\mathcal{L}_{int}=-\frac{1}{4\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}+j_{\mu}A_{\mu} $

+ **若光子质量不为零：**
    - **拉格朗日量密度发生改变：**

  $ \mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{f}+\mathcal{L}_{int}=-\frac{1}{4\mu_{0}}F_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}+j_{\mu}A_{\mu}-\frac{\mu^{2}}{2\mu_{0}}A_{\mu}A_{\mu} $

    -   $ \mu=\frac{m_{0}c}{\hbar} $具有波矢量纲，$ m_{0} $ 为假设的光子质量。
    - 电磁场张量发生改变：

$ F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_{\mu}A_{\nu}-\partial_{\nu}A_{\mu} $

    - 电磁场方程改变：
        * 取洛伦兹规范$ \partial_{\mu}A_{\mu}=0 $：

$ \Rightarrow(\Box-\mu^{2})A_{\mu}=-\mu_{0}j_{\mu} $

    - 规范对称性被破坏：

  $ A_{\mu}\rightarrow A_{\mu}^{\prime}=A_{\mu}+\partial_{\mu}\Lambda $  
  $ F_{\mu\nu}\rightarrow F_{\mu\nu}^{\prime}=F_{\mu\nu} $  
  $ A_{\mu}^{\prime}A_{\mu}^{\prime}\ne A_{\mu}A_{\mu} $

> 光子质量项破坏规范不变性，因此光子质量为零。
>
